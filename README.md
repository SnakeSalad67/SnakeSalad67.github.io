# SnakeSalad67.github.io

## Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026



## Overview
This app displays your approximate GPS coordinates on screen. That's it. This policy explains how location data is handled.



## Location Data
The app requests access to your device's location solely to display your approximate coordinates. Your location is not collected, stored, transmitted, or sold by the developer.
Location data is used only within the app, on your device, in real time, and is discarded when the app is closed.



## Third-Party Services
This app is distributed via Google Play. Google may collect diagnostic, usage, or analytics data as part of operating the Play platform. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one.


## Analytics
Basic, anonymous analytics (such as app crash reports or install counts) may be collected by Google Play Services as part of standard platform operation. The developer does not receive personally identifiable information through these channels.



## Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.
